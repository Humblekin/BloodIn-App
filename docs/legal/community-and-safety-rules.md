# BloodIn Community and Safety Rules

**DRAFT - NOT READY TO PUBLISH**

These rules apply to profiles, blood requests, responses, messages, voice
notes, posts, comments, communities, and organization workflows.

## Our core safety rules

1. **Never buy or sell blood.** Do not offer money or other benefits in
   exchange for blood or a donation. BloodIn Premium is not a blood service.
2. **Use qualified medical facilities.** Donation eligibility, testing,
   screening, and transfusion must be determined by qualified professionals.
   BloodIn does not provide medical advice or emergency response.
3. **Be truthful.** Do not make fake requests, impersonate a patient, donor,
   facility, or health worker, or misrepresent blood type, location,
   availability, or donation history.
4. **Protect private information.** Share only information you have authority
   to provide. Do not post someone's health details, phone number, exact
   location, identity documents, or private messages without permission or
   lawful authority.
5. **Treat people respectfully.** No harassment, threats, discrimination,
   coercion, sexual exploitation, or intimidation.
6. **Use location carefully.** Do not use BloodIn to stalk, track, or
   approach someone who has not agreed to meet. Arrange any meeting in a safe
   public place and involve a trusted person when appropriate.
7. **Use staff access responsibly.** Organization owners, administrators,
   and staff may access information only for their assigned work. They must
   not search for acquaintances, share records, or use BloodIn information
   for unrelated purposes.
8. **Keep the service secure.** Do not share passwords, bypass permissions,
   scrape personal data, or attempt to disrupt the service.

## Reporting, blocking, and enforcement

Use the in-app report and block features where available. Include enough
information for a reviewer to locate the conduct, but avoid sharing more
sensitive information than necessary. Contact **0599963155** for platform
safety concerns. This is not an emergency response line. BloodIn may review
reports and restrict content or accounts under its final Terms and applicable
law.

The current app includes report/block-related functionality, but these rules
do not promise that every item is proactively reviewed or that a report will
receive an immediate response. For medical emergencies, contact local
emergency services or a hospital directly.

## If you believe someone is in immediate danger

Contact local emergency services or a qualified medical facility. BloodIn is
not an emergency service and may not see or act on a request immediately.
