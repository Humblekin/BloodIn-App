This Notice explains how the operator of BloodIn ("BloodIn", "we", "us") uses
personal information in the BloodIn mobile application and staff portal. It is
a draft based on the current code, not legal advice. Do not publish it until
the placeholders, actual production configuration, retention rules, and legal
review are complete.

## 1. What BloodIn does

BloodIn helps people, donors, and health-related organizations connect about
blood donation needs. It is a coordination platform. It is not a hospital,
blood bank, medical practitioner, emergency service, or substitute for
professional medical advice, screening, diagnosis, treatment, or transfusion
services.

## 2. Information we handle

Depending on how you use BloodIn, we may handle:

- **Account and profile information:** email address, display name, profile
  details, profile image, account identifiers, and authentication information.
  Supabase Auth handles passwords; BloodIn's application code does not receive
  or store a user's plaintext password.
- **Blood and donation-related information:** blood group, donation
  willingness/availability, blood requests and responses, donation
  acknowledgements or records, donor-card information, and related facility
  records. This can reveal information about a person's health and must be
  treated as sensitive.
- **Location information:** when you grant foreground location permission,
  BloodIn can obtain device coordinates, resolve a nearby city/region, and
  store current coordinates and locality information for nearby matching.
  The app can refresh a saved current location while relevant screens are
  used. Nearby features can calculate and display approximate distance.
  Location visibility settings control what location detail other users can
  see; they do not mean BloodIn itself stops processing location needed for
  the feature.
- **Requests and communications:** request descriptions, contact numbers
  supplied with requests, responses, text messages, voice-note media and
  metadata, and information needed to deliver in-app notifications. Request
  contact numbers are restricted in the database to the requester and people
  who respond to that request. Voice notes are stored in a private storage
  bucket and are intended to be accessible to conversation participants.
- **Community and safety information:** posts, comments, community
  participation, reports, blocks, and moderation or audit records.
- **Organization and workforce information:** facility details, staff account
  and membership information, role/permission assignments, and donation
  records handled through authorized facility workflows.
- **Payment information:** premium transaction reference, amount, currency,
  and transaction status. Checkout is initiated with Paystack. Payment card
  details are entered with Paystack, not directly into BloodIn's app code.
  Paystack's own terms and privacy notice also apply to its processing.
- **Technical and security information:** authentication/session data,
  service requests, and security or operational records generated by the
  services used to operate the app. The exact logs and retention periods
  depend on production configuration.

BloodIn's current sign-up form does not ask for a date of birth or
independently verify age. It collects an email address, display name, and
password and asks users to confirm they are at least 18 and acknowledge that
BloodIn is not a medical-services provider.

## 3. Why we use information

Subject to applicable Ghanaian law, we use information to:

- create and secure accounts and provide BloodIn features;
- show relevant donors, requests, organizations, and approximate proximity;
- facilitate donation coordination and communications;
- maintain user privacy preferences and deliver requested in-app
  notifications;
- operate organization and staff workflows, including authorized donation
  recording;
- process and verify premium purchases;
- prevent fraud, abuse, unsafe conduct, and unauthorized access;
- respond to support, privacy, safety, and legal requests; and
- meet applicable legal obligations and protect the rights and safety of
  users and others.

We will identify and document the lawful grounds and, where required, obtain
valid consent for sensitive personal information before production use.
Consent can be withdrawn where consent is the applicable ground; withdrawal
does not undo processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

## 4. How information may be shared

We may make information available:

- to other BloodIn users according to the feature, your privacy settings, and
  the information you choose to post or send;
- to requesters and people who respond to their blood requests, including
  request contact information as described above;
- to authorized owners, administrators, and staff of an organization for
  organization workflows, subject to their permissions and applicable law;
- to Supabase, which provides the configured authentication, database,
  storage, and Edge Function infrastructure;
- to Paystack to initiate and verify payments;
- to device operating systems and browser location services when you use
  location-enabled features;
- to vendors needed to operate the service, once identified and disclosed in
  the production vendor list; and
- where required by law, valid legal process, or an urgent need to protect
  someone from serious harm, subject to applicable law.

The exact Supabase hosting region, subprocessors, email-delivery provider, and
international transfer destinations have not been verified for this draft.
They must be confirmed from the production accounts before this Notice is
published. We do not claim that BloodIn uses end-to-end encrypted messaging.

## 5. Visibility and your controls

The app currently provides settings for blood-group visibility, location
visibility, who may message you, discovery and Orbit visibility, and several
notification preferences. You can also block users. These controls affect
specified app features; they do not remove information already shared with
another person, copied outside BloodIn, or retained where required for safety,
legal, or operational reasons.

Profile avatars are stored in a public Supabase Storage bucket. Do not upload
an image that you are not comfortable being accessible by its public URL.
Voice notes use a private bucket with participant access policies.

## 6. Retention and deletion

BloodIn has not yet set a complete, published retention schedule. We will
retain information only for as long as a documented purpose and applicable
law require, and then delete or de-identify it where appropriate. Exact
periods for each record type must be decided and implemented before
publication.

The current database deletion design does not erase every shared record when
an account is removed. Some messages, request responses, shared organization
records, reports, moderation records, and audit history can remain with the
former user's identity link set to null; other related data may be removed
through database cascades. Backups and vendor logs may have separate deletion
cycles. This description must be reconciled with production behavior.

The current app source does not identify a self-service account deletion
feature. Until a tested process is available, contact **0599963155** to
request account deletion or exercise a privacy right. We will verify the
request and explain any information we must retain and why. Replace this
temporary instruction with a tested process and response timeframe before
publication.

## 7. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access or correct information about
you, request deletion or restriction, object to certain uses, withdraw
consent, or raise a concern about our handling of your information. Contact
**0599963155**. We may need to verify your identity and may ask for
details needed to locate the relevant records.

You may also contact the Ghana Data Protection Commission:
<https://dpc.gov.gh/>. The operator must confirm its registration and all
applicable duties under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), before
launch.

## 8. Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the service,
including authenticated access controls. No online service can guarantee
absolute security. We do not claim that all messages or data are
end-to-end-encrypted. The operator must maintain and test access reviews,
incident response, backup, staff training, and vendor security procedures.

If you believe your account or information has been accessed improperly,
contact **0599963155** promptly.

## 9. Children and age requirement

BloodIn is intended only for people aged **18 or older**. Registration
requires an 18-or-older self-attestation, but this is not independent age
verification. If we learn that an underage person has provided information,
contact **0599963155**.
