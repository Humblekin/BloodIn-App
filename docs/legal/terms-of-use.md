## 3. What BloodIn is - and is not

BloodIn is a coordination platform that helps users and health-related
organizations communicate about blood donation needs. BloodIn does not:

- provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, blood testing, screening,
  transfusion, or emergency response;
- determine whether a person is medically eligible to donate;
- certify that a donor, request, facility, blood supply, or medical claim is
  accurate or safe; or
- guarantee that a donor, blood product, facility, response, or outcome will
  be available.

For medical decisions, eligibility, screening, and transfusion, use a
qualified health professional and an appropriate licensed facility. In an
emergency, contact local emergency services or a hospital directly; do not
rely on BloodIn.

## 4. Donation safety and prohibited transactions

Blood must not be bought or sold through BloodIn. You must not request,
offer, arrange, or facilitate payment or other commercial exchange for blood.
The app's Premium payment, if offered, is payment for the stated digital
features only and never for blood, donors, medical treatment, or priority
access to a donation.

All donation decisions and procedures must take place through qualified
medical professionals and appropriate facilities. Users must not treat a
profile, blood-group label, donor card, location match, or prior donation
record as a substitute for clinical checks.

BloodIn provides tools to create requests, respond to requests, exchange text
and voice messages, and participate in communities. A request may disclose
information to other users. Contact details attached to a request are
available to the requester and users who respond to that request, subject to
the current database rules. Review your content before posting.

Privacy settings can limit some profile visibility, discovery, location
detail, and messaging. They cannot control screenshots, copying, or
disclosures by recipients outside BloodIn. Do not rely on a privacy setting
to conceal information you cannot safely share.
